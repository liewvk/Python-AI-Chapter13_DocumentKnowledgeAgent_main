BRIGHTWOOD LEARNING CENTRE - LIBRARY FAQ
Fictional example for AI education. Not an actual school policy.

Opening hours: The student library opens Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM, except public holidays.
Borrowing limit: Each student may borrow up to three books at a time for fourteen calendar days.
Renewals: A student may renew a borrowed book once if another student has not reserved it.
Late returns: Students should contact the librarian if a book cannot be returned by its due date. The sample policy does not specify a late fee.
Lost books: The student should notify the librarian. The librarian explains the replacement process.
Contact: Ask the library desk during normal opening hours.
