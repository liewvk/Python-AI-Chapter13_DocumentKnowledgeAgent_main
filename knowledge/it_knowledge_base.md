# Brightwood Learning Centre — IT Knowledge Base

*Fictional sample. Not an official policy.*

## Password Reset
Students who cannot sign in to the learning portal must visit the IT help desk with their student ID card. The help desk will confirm their identity and provide instructions to reset the password. Students must not email passwords to support staff.

## Wi-Fi Access
The student wireless network is named **Brightwood-Student**. Students sign in using their school network accounts. Guests must request a temporary visitor account from reception.

## Support Hours
The IT help desk is open on school days from **9:00 AM to 5:00 PM**. Routine support tickets normally receive a first response within **two working days**.

## Data Protection
Students should not upload confidential school records to unknown public AI services. The example RAG project is designed for fictional reference documents only.
